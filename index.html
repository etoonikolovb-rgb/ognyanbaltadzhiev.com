About

Engineer on board, focused on power and propulsion

I'm a Ship's Electrical Engineer with a Certificate of Competency as Electro-Technical Officer (STCW III/6). Since 2023 I've served as 1st Electrical Engineer with Silversea Cruises: on the expedition ship Silver Endeavour (Azipod, ABB 800xA), the dual-fuel Silver Ray (Wärtsilä 31DF, VEM propulsion motors, Valmet DNA), Silver Whisper and Silver Dawn.

Before cruise ships I sailed as Electrical Officer on bulk carriers (Wärtsilä Sulzer RT-flex and MAN B&W ME-B main engines) and on the 70,000 GT diesel-electric cruise ship Ambience.

My work covers power generation and LV/HV distribution, diesel-electric propulsion, automation and alarm systems, troubleshooting, and planned maintenance.

I follow where ship power systems are going. During my Master's at TU Varna I studied onboard DC grids in depth: ABB's Onboard DC Grid, solid-state DC breakers, and the efficiency gains for variable-speed operation.

In my own time I build tools that turn a ship's drawings, manuals and alarm lists into an indexed, searchable library. Every result points back to the original drawing and page, the originals are never changed, and the tools work offline. The aim is simple: find the right sensor, setpoint or circuit in minutes during a real alarm, using modern search, including AI-assisted search, while the engineer and the original documents stay in charge.
